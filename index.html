Biomedical polymers · Biocompatibility & hybrid materials

Where synthetic polymers meet biological machinery.

We design stimuli-responsive polymers and protein–polymer hybrid materials that sense their environment and act on it: releasing genes, running enzymatic cascades, and adapting to cues from living systems.

Research at a glance

Five connected lines of work, unified by one question: how do we build materials that read their environment and respond with function?

Protein–polymer hybrid materials

Bioconjugation strategies bridging polymer chemistry with structural biology.

Stimuli-responsive nanogels

Networks that swell, collapse, or disassemble on cue to release their cargo.

CRISPR / Cas9 RNP delivery

Protecting and delivering gene-editing machinery across mucosal barriers.

Chemoenzymatic nanoreactors

Enzyme-loaded nanogels running cascade reactions in confinement.

Theranostics & digital twins

Physics-informed models predicting how a nanocarrier will behave.

CRISPLab · Programming responsive CRISPR hybrid materials

Materials that sense, stabilize, and steer CRISPR.

CRISPLab designs stimuli-responsive and hybrid materials purpose-built around CRISPR/Cas9 — from single-particle engineering and conjugation chemistry to sensing, stabilization, and delivery across biological barriers.

Research at a glance

Six connected lines of work, all built around one goal: programming responsive materials for CRISPR.

CRISPR / Cas9 RNP delivery

Protecting and delivering gene-editing machinery across mucosal barriers.

Single-particle engineering

Controlling payload, size, and surface chemistry one particle at a time.

CRISPR-responsive sensing

Materials that sense editing activity and report or trigger on it.

CRISPR conjugation strategies

Site-selective chemistry linking polymers directly to Cas9 or sgRNA.

Small-molecule stabilization

Additives and modifications that protect RNP activity in storage.

Digital twins for CRISPR delivery

Physics-informed models predicting nanocarrier behavior.
